From Tommy and Laurene

Why We Wrote These Guides

We are Tommy and Laurene, a husband and wife wedding photography team, and we have been photographing weddings all over Northern Ireland since 2014. Some venues we have been back to again and again. We have seen them on bright summer afternoons, in pouring rain, in the middle of winter and everything in between.

When you are planning a wedding, honest information about venues is surprisingly hard to find. Venue websites and brochures show every place at its very best, which is exactly what they should do. What they do not tell you is where the light is tricky, how far the best photo spots are from the drinks reception, or what really happens when the weather turns.

That is why we put these guides together. Each one covers a venue we know well, with the things we love about it and the things we think are worth knowing before you book. It is simply our own view from working there, the good and the not so good.

We hope they help you find the right place for your day. If you have a question about any of these venues, or one that is not on the list yet, just get in touch. We are always happy to chat.
