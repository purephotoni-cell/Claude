What makes a great wedding venue

Beautiful rooms matter, but the things that make the biggest difference on the day are often the ones nobody mentions on the show round.

Every guide on this page looks at the same four things. They are the questions we find couples wish they had asked before booking, and the ones that shape how your day feels and how your photos turn out.

  • 01

    Is it all yours?

    Some venues host one wedding a day and some host two or more. Neither is wrong, but it changes how private your day feels and how much of the grounds you can use for photos.

  • 02

    What happens if it rains?

    In Northern Ireland this matters more than almost anything. We look at where your ceremony, drinks and group photos move to, and whether that plan B still looks good in your photos.

  • 03

    How close are the best photo spots?

    The loveliest portrait spot is no use if it is a long walk away. We note how far the good spots are from your guests and where the light works best, so you are not missing your own drinks reception.

  • 04

    Where does everyone stay?

    Rooms on site make the morning and the end of the night far easier, especially for guests travelling from further away.
