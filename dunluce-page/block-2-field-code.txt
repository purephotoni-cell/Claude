<h2>The elopement field <em>facing Dunluce Castle</em></h2>
<p class="pn-dl2-pull">Most couples expect to marry inside the castle. The ceremony actually happens on a private clifftop field right across from it, and that is where the view is.</p>
</div>
<div>
<p class="pn-dl2-p">Dunluce Castle itself is a busy tourist spot, so elopement ceremonies are not held inside the ruins. Instead you marry on the edge of the cliff in a field owned by local farmer Sean McKinley, looking straight across at the castle with the sea below.</p>
<p class="pn-dl2-p">Sean is one of the friendliest people you could meet and he loves helping couples make this happen. The field is yours alone for your time slot, which matters on a coast that welcomes hundreds of thousands of visitors every year.
