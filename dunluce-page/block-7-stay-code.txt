<ul class="pn-dl7-list">

<li><b>In Belfast</b><span>Plenty of couples stay in Belfast and travel up on the day. As our largest city it has the biggest choice of places to stay, and it makes a great base for the rest of your trip.</span></li>

<h3>More photo spots nearby</h3>
<ul class="pn-dl7-list">

<li><b>Dunseverick Castle</b><span>The quiet ruins of an old castle on the clifftop, also just a few miles along the coast. A lovely calm contrast after the drama of Dunluce.</span></li>
<li><b>The Dark Hedges</b><span>The famous avenue of twisted beech trees, a short drive inland. It is busiest in the middle of the day, so early morning or evening works best.</span></li>
