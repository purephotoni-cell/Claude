<p class="pn-dl5-eyebrow">The day itself</p>
<h2>What a Dunluce elopement day <em>looks like</em></h2>
<p class="pn-dl5-p">Every elopement is different, but most follow a similar shape. Here is an example of a relaxed six hour day, along with our honest advice on when to come and what the weather might do.</p>
</div>
<div class="pn-dl5-grid">
<div>
<h3>An example six hour day</h3>
<ol class="pn-dl5-time">
<li><b>Early afternoon</b><span>We arrive while you are getting ready and capture the calm before it all starts.</span></li>
<li><b>Mid afternoon</b><span>Sean meets you and your guests and drives you out to the field.</span></li>
<li><b>The ceremony</b><span>You say your vows on the clifftop with Dunluce Castle behind you.</span></li>
<li><b>Straight after</b><span>Relaxed portraits on the field while everyone is still buzzing.</span></li>
<li><b>Back at the cottage</b><span>A shot of whiskey with Sean to celebrate.</span></li>
<li><b>Early evening</b><span>A short drive to Ballintoy Harbour or Dunseverick Castle for more portraits, both only a few miles away.</span></li>
<li><b>To finish</b><span>Dinner together, with us there for the first toast if you would like.</span></li>
</ol>
<p class="pn-dl5-small">Your ceremony time depends on the slots available at the field, so we plan the rest of the day around it together.</p>
</div>
<div>
<h3>When to come and what to expect</h3>
<div class="pn-dl5-cards">
<div class="pn-dl5-card"><h4>The most popular months</h4><p>Most elopements here happen between March and November. Summer gives you around 17 hours of daylight, while winter has closer to 8, so the time of year shapes how much you can fit in.</p></div>
<div class="pn-dl5-card"><h4>The light</h4><p>Golden hour, just after sunrise or before sunset, gives the softest light. The only time we try to avoid is the middle of the day when the sun is highest and the shadows are harsh.</p></div>
<div class="pn-dl5-card"><h4>The weather</h4><p>You can get four seasons in one day here, even in summer. The good news is that our rain tends to come as showers rather than all day downpours, so there are nearly always dry spells to work with.</p></div>
<div class="pn-dl5-card"><h4>Mist and cloud</h4><p>Some of our favourite Dunluce photos were taken in thick sea mist. A grey day makes the castle look moody and dramatic, so please do not panic if the forecast is not perfect.</p></div>
</div>
