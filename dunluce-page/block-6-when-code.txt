<h2>Seasons, light and <em>Irish weather</em></h2>
<p class="pn-dl6-p">There is no perfect time to elope at Dunluce, only the right time for you. Here is our honest advice after years of photographing on this coast in every kind of weather.</p>
<figure class="pn-dl6-pic"><img src="https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/53707517e4b054112dbfd825/9afec81f-bc9e-4ad6-aace-ecbfdad8809f/Golden+Hour+Sunset+photography+at+Dunluce+Castle+Elopement?format=1000w" alt="Bride and groom walking along the coast at golden hour after their Dunluce Castle elopement" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Golden hour portraits on the Causeway Coast</figcaption></figure>
</div>
<div class="pn-dl6-cards">
<div class="pn-dl6-card"><span>01</span><h3>The most popular months</h3><p>Most elopements here happen between March and November. Summer gives you around 17 hours of daylight, while winter has closer to 8, so the time of year shapes how much you can fit into your day.</p></div>
<div class="pn-dl6-card"><span>02</span><h3>The light</h3><p>Golden hour, just after sunrise or before sunset, gives the softest light. The only time we try to avoid is the middle of the day when the sun is highest and the shadows are harsh.</p></div>
<div class="pn-dl6-card"><span>03</span><h3>The weather</h3><p>You can get four seasons in one day here, even in summer. The good news is that our rain tends to come as showers rather than all day downpours, so there are nearly always dry spells to work with.</p></div>
<div class="pn-dl6-card"><span>04</span><h3>Mist and cloud</h3><p>Some of our favourite Dunluce photos were taken in thick sea mist. A grey day makes the castle look moody and dramatic, so please do not panic if the forecast is not perfect.</p></div>
